Photoshop is a photo editing, digital art, and graphic design software program 
It was created in 1987 by Thomas and John Knoll and acquired by Adobe in 1988 
Initially created as a tool to show grayscale images on monochrome screens 
Has tools for mostly photo manipulation, as well as digital painting, layer blending, vector drawing, and generative AI features 
Subscription-only software through Adobe Creative Cloud 
Works on Windows, macOS, and iPadOS 
"Photoshop" became a verb for manipulating images 
It supports a lot of file formats, brush types, and colors
