Audacity is a free audio editor and recorder
It was created in 1999 by Dominic Mazzoni and Roger Dannenberg at Carnegie Mellon University 
It was initially built as a basic audio analyzer for college research 
It has tools for recording audio, trimming tracks, removing background noise, and applying effects 
It is free under the GNU General Public License 
Works on Windows, macOS, and Linux 
It is a popular open-source audio tool for podcasting and quick voiceovers

https://www.audacityteam.org/
