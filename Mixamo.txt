Mixamo is a web-based 3D platform used to rig and animate 3D humanoid characters 
It was founded in 2008 by Stefano Corazza and Miro Kirov at Stanford University and acquired by Adobe in 2015 
It was initially created to sell individual motion-capture animations to game developers online 
It features an automatic character rigger and a library of thousands of pre-made motion-capture animations 
It is free to use with an Adobe account 
Works on any modern web browser 
It reduced the barrier to entry for solo developers who don't know how to manually rig or animate 3D models 
It provides animations in FBX and Collada formats compatible with every major 3D engine and suite
