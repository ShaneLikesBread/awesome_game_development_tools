Blender is a free 3D creation software mostly used for modeling and animation
It was released in 1994 by Dutch animation studio NeoGeo and is now maintained by the Blender Foundation 
It started as an in-house tool before being saved from bankruptcy
It includes features for 3D modeling, sculpting, 2D animation, and video editing 
It is a free software with no subscriptions or feature locks 
Works on Windows, macOS, and Linux 
It has been used to create scenes and effects in major feature films, indie games, and short films 
It has multiple render engines
